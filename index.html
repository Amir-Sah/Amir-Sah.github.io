About

I'm Amir, a game designer from Iran, now living in Cologne and in my third semester at Cologne Game Lab.

I trained as a computer engineer, so I design and I build. Between game projects I have worked as a back-end developer and a data analyst, and I now work part-time alongside my master's.

My design practice comes from shipping mobile games with a small studio and from years of designing play for children. I'm most interested in game-based learning, narrative design and the cultural impact of games.
