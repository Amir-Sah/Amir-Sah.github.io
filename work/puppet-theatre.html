Puppet theatre series

A funny, educational puppet show in seven episodes, each written around that week's teaching plan.

My role
Writer, video editor, performer
Episodes
Seven
Audience
Ages 5 to 8
For
Yarigaran-e Mehr
Language
Persian

Show, then talk

Each episode was projected in a hall for the children's programme. Afterwards we talked with the children about it and checked how well the message had come across.

The topics followed the programme's weekly plan. The Nowruz episode, for example, was about the ancient Persian New Year and its traditions.

Wrote all seven episodes from the weekly teaching plan.
