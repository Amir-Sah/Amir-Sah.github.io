Heroes of the Island

An island cut in two

One half is safe and has a hospital. The other half is in ruins. Three routes connect them: a broken bridge, a road covered in rubble and a tunnel that still has to be dug.

Four roles can each clear one kind of obstacle, but only if the group brought the right equipment. The game is about deciding together, before you know exactly what you'll face.

Pick four of twenty

Before play, each team ticks four items on its sheet. That choice decides which obstacles they can clear later.

During play, teams collect coins and ability cards. An ability keeps you safe from attack for one round, or adds a die to your next attack or defence.

Worksheets and memory games

Smaller activities for the same programme: drawing, matching and memory games, printed for each session and taken home.

All sheets are in Persian. Most illustrations are clip art; the activities and layouts are designed for the programme.

The programme

Yarigarane Mehr is an independent, donation-funded charity that I co-founded in 2017.

Since 2024 its children's programme has met weekly at a local cultural centre, in three age groups, with about 100 children in total. We facilitate rather than lecture: games, animations and discussion, with a lot of listening.

A four-hour session takes about ten hours of planning, and every mentor reports back afterwards. I still design for the programme from Cologne and join some sessions online.
