Game jams

Two small games I made solo, in a few days each, both about making a decision under pressure. Both are playable in the browser.

Rush Mush Flip

Platformer / GMTK Game Jam 2026 / solo, made in four days

The gap between floor and ceiling keeps shrinking, and your only input is SPACE.

Pressing it flips gravity so you can dodge spikes from either side. But a flip also mirrors your countdown: the hourglass shows how much time you have now and how much you would have if you flipped. Every flip is a bet against your own clock.

Play on itch.io

Subject Null

Interactive fiction / Brackeys Game Jam 2026.2 / solo

Is the person in front of you human or machine?

You question a subject against their personnel file. Some answers can be checked with a scan, but you only have a few actions, so you choose which claims are worth verifying before you decide.
